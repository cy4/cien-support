Beginner Spanish, spoken out loud

Support

Last updated October 22, 2026

Dilo is a beginner Spanish speaking tutor for iPhone and iPad. You hear a Spanish voice, say the phrase back, and get a score for how closely your words match.

The microphone and speech recognition

The first time you practice out loud, the iPhone asks for the microphone and for speech recognition. Dilo needs both so it can hear you and turn that into words.

If you said no, open the Settings app, tap Dilo, and turn Microphone and Speech Recognition on. Until those are allowed, Dilo can still play the phrase, but it cannot score what you say.

What the score means

Dilo listens, turns your speech into words, and compares those words with the phrase. The number is a speaking coach: a nudge for practice, not a lab grade of your accent or a phonetics test.

A best score of 90 or higher counts as mastered. Below that, the phrase is still worth another try.

The yearly subscriptions

Dilo has two auto-renewing yearly subscriptions. In the US, each is $1.99 a year. The app shows Apple's current price for your region before you confirm.

Dilo Yearly (No Trial) charges right away. You get full access to everything immediately.

Dilo Yearly (7 Day Trial) starts with a 7-day Trial Period. During the Trial Period, Browse and Practice share 25 different words or phrases a day. Saying one again does not use another try. Looking through the list does not count. The count resets at local midnight. At 25, new words wait until the next local midnight. During the Trial Period, Unlock now switches you to Dilo Yearly (No Trial), charges right away, and removes the limit. When the 7 days end, Apple charges the yearly price, and then everything is unlocked.

Without a subscription, Dilo stays on the unlock screen. Your progress stays on the device. After you subscribe, Apple bills the yearly price again each year until you cancel.

How to cancel

Dilo cannot cancel the subscription for you. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name at the top (your Apple ID), then tap Subscriptions. Choose Dilo Yearly (No Trial) or Dilo Yearly (7 Day Trial) and cancel. Cancel before the Trial Period ends to avoid the charge. You keep access until the Trial Period, or the year you already started, comes to an end.

Restore Purchases

Reinstalled Dilo, or moved to another iPhone? Open Settings inside Dilo and tap Restore Purchases. The same button is on the unlock screen. It asks Apple for Dilo Yearly (No Trial) or Dilo Yearly (7 Day Trial) on this Apple ID, so use the Apple ID that started the subscription.

Review reminders

Review reminders are on for a new install. The first time Dilo opens, the iPhone asks permission to send notifications. If you say no, Dilo turns reminders off. To turn them back on later, allow notifications for Dilo in the iPhone Settings app.

Under Which words, choose Random, Needs improvement, or In order. Change reminder notification interval to space them closer together, a bit further apart, much further apart, or rarely.

Reminder categories are saved on their own. Choose All, or any mix of categories. Changing categories in Practice or Browse leaves this list as you set it. Turn every category off and Dilo does not schedule alerts.

Alerts include words you have not tried and words that still need work in the categories you leave on. Mastered words stay quiet. Nothing is sent from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. A reminder due then waits until 8:00 a.m. Each alert shows the Spanish and the English meaning. Tap it to open that word in Practice.

Turn Review reminders off in Settings when you want them to stop.

Reset stats

In Settings, tap Reset stats and confirm. This clears practiced and mastered scores, including the words Dilo heard. Your other settings stay as they are.
