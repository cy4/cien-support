Getting started

I am new to learning Spanish. Where do I start?

Start with the lessons.

  1. From lessons, select "Start Here: Beginner Path".
  2. Complete the lessons in order, and take the Quiz at the end of each lesson.
  3. After you finish the lessons in a section, do "Practice this stage".

"Practice this stage" reinforces your progress. It saves your place, so the next time you come back you can pick up where you left off. The stats underneath the "Practice this stage" button for that section show your progress.

Speaking and pronunciation

The microphone and speech recognition

The first time you practice out loud, the iPhone asks for the microphone and for speech recognition. Dilo needs both so it can hear you and turn that into words.

If you said no:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Dilo.
  3. Turn Microphone and Speech Recognition on.

Until those are allowed, Dilo can still play the phrase, but it cannot score what you say.

I am not being heard

Make sure background noise is not interfering with listening, and that your microphone is not blocked. Practice with your mouth closer to the phone. Speak clearly.

If Dilo still does not hear you, you may not be pronouncing the word the way the app expects. Try a few more times, or move on to another word and come back.

How do I know why a word was scored wrong?

Turn on "Detailed Scoring" in Settings. Dilo shows what Apple Speech Recognition heard, the individual sounds it heard, and how well you pronounced them.

Can Dilo listen again after I answer?

Yes.

  1. In Settings, turn on "Speak then automatically listen for a response".
  2. Set the threshold below which Dilo listens again.

If you set that threshold to zero, Dilo does not listen again. It listens only the first time.

Mastery and progress

What the score means

Dilo listens, turns your speech into words, and compares those words with the phrase. The number is a speaking coach: a nudge for practice, not a lab grade of your accent or a phonetics test.

I scored over 90 and was not marked Mastered. How does Mastered work?

Mastery is decided for one session, on that word or phrase. Within one session, the average of your back-to-back attempts must be over 90. If you try a word three times in a row and only the last attempt is over 90, that is not enough. The average of those three attempts has to be over 90.

To start that count over, go to another word and then come back to the one you want to master.

I practiced a mastered word again and did poorly. Do I lose Mastered?

No. Once you master a word or phrase, it stays mastered unless you reset all stats. A later poor attempt does not remove it.

How do I see the words I have mastered or practiced?

  1. From the Home screen, scroll down to the "Mastered" count or the "Practiced" count.
  2. Tap it. Dilo opens that list in the browse view.
  3. To clear the "Mastered" or "Practiced" filter, select "Show all".

Reset stats

To reset all stats, open Settings, tap Reset stats, and confirm. This clears practiced and mastered scores, including the words Dilo heard. Your other settings stay as they are.

Dictionary and filters

I cannot find a word in the Dictionary. Why?

The dictionary is meant to help on a beginner or intermediate Spanish learning journey. Some words are not included, and the one you want may not be there.

A filter may also be on. Clear it by selecting "ALL" in categories, and "ALL" in the word types line near the top of the page.

Why don't filters in Browse or Practice carry over?

Each view has its own filters, so you can leave a view and pick up there later. Set the filters yourself in every view.

Notifications

What are Spaced Repetition Notifications?

They nudge you through the day with the words and phrases you want to learn. Spaced repetition helps people retain new information.

In Settings you choose the interval and the filters. You can be notified for only mastered words, or only words that need practice. You can narrow that by category or by word type: Word, Phrase, Idiom, or Slang. Slang requires an additional license be purchased.

Spaced Repetition Notifications are on for a new install. The first time Dilo opens, the iPhone asks permission to send notifications. If you say no, Dilo turns them off. To turn them on later, allow notifications for Dilo in the iPhone Settings app.

To set them:

  1. In Settings, turn on "Spaced Repetition Notifications" if you want the notifications.
  2. Select the "Which words" filter you need. For example, select Idioms.
  3. Select the categories you want to be notified about.

If the word type you want has no words or phrases in the categories you selected, you will not get notifications. Notification categories are saved on their own. Changing categories in Practice or Browse leaves this list as you set it.

Each alert shows the Spanish and the English meaning. Tap it to open that word in Practice.

How do I change how often notifications arrive?

  1. In Settings, find "Change Spaced Repetition Notification interval".
  2. Tap + or - to raise or lower the interval.

There are four intervals. Dilo never sends a notification between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM.

Purchases

The yearly subscriptions

Dilo has two auto-renewing yearly subscriptions. In the US, each is $1.99 a year. The app shows Apple's current price for your region before you confirm.

Dilo Yearly (No Trial) charges right away. You get full access to everything immediately.

Dilo Yearly (7 Day Trial) starts with a 7-day Trial Period. During the Trial Period, Browse and Practice share 25 different words or phrases a day. Saying one again does not use another try. Looking through the list does not count. The count resets at local midnight. At 25, new words wait until the next local midnight. During the Trial Period, Unlock now switches you to Dilo Yearly (No Trial), charges right away, and removes the limit. When the 7 days end, Apple charges the yearly price, and then everything is unlocked.

Without a subscription, Dilo stays on the unlock screen. Your progress stays on the device. After you subscribe, Apple bills the yearly price again each year until you cancel.

How to cancel

Dilo cannot cancel the subscription for you. On your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top (your Apple ID).
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Choose Dilo Yearly (No Trial) or Dilo Yearly (7 Day Trial) and cancel.

Cancel before the Trial Period ends to avoid the charge. You keep access until the Trial Period, or the year you already started, comes to an end.

I purchased a subscription and can no longer use it. How do I restore access?

Reinstalled Dilo, or moved to another iPhone?

  1. When you open Dilo, tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the screen. The same button is on the unlock screen.
  2. If you are already in the app, open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases".

It asks Apple for Dilo Yearly (No Trial) or Dilo Yearly (7 Day Trial) on this Apple ID, so use the Apple ID that started the subscription.

Slang Pack

Dilo is an 18+ app for adults only. The Slang Pack contains slang and may include coarse and foul language. Slang requires an additional license be purchased.

How do I show or hide crude and vulgar slang?

In Settings, select the switch for "Show crude & vulgar slang". You can turn it off to hide that content. This option is enforced by the age set on your device.
