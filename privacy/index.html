Dilo for iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy covers the Dilo app, bundle id com.cien.spanish (the app’s internal ID, kept from its earlier name). Dilo does not ask you to make an account. Your practice stays on your device. The developer does not run a server for the app, and the app has no analytics.

No account

Dilo does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. There is no sign-in. There is no advertising, and the app does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting kit.

What stays on this device

Practice and settings are saved in the app’s local storage on the iPhone or iPad. That includes:

  • which phrases you have tried, how many times, your best and latest scores, and the words Dilo heard
  • settings such as the Spanish voice, playback speed, dark theme, review reminders, and the reminder categories you chose
  • which phrases counted toward today’s trial limit
  • when the current subscription period ends, and whether that period is the free trial

Dilo does not sync this through iCloud or send it to the developer. Removing the app removes it from that device.

Microphone and speech

The microphone and speech recognition are used only to score practice. Dilo plays a phrase with the iPhone’s own voice, then listens while you say it back. The recording is not saved. The words Dilo heard are saved with your score, on the device.

When the device supports on-device speech recognition, Dilo requires that and the audio stays on the device. When it does not, Apple’s speech recognition service may process the audio to produce the text. That processing is Apple’s, under Apple’s privacy policy. Dilo does not send the audio, the transcript, or the score to the developer.

Reminders

Review reminders are on for a new install. You can turn them off in Settings. They are local notifications scheduled on your device, not messages from a server. Reminder categories are saved on the device, on their own: All, or any mix. That list is separate from Practice and Browse. Dilo does not schedule a reminder from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. One that is due then waits until 8:00 a.m. A reminder shows a Spanish phrase and its English meaning so you can tap it and open that word in Practice. If reminders are off, Dilo does not schedule them. Dilo does not send any other notifications.

Purchases

The yearly subscription is sold by Apple through StoreKit. Dilo never sees your card number or your Apple ID password. Apple tells the app whether the subscription is active, when it ends, and whether the current period is the free trial. Restore Purchases asks Apple to sync purchases for the Apple ID on this device. Apple’s privacy policy covers the payment.

Children

Dilo is a 4+ app for a general audience. It does not ask a child, or anyone else, to create an account or to give a name, email, photo, or location. The developer does not collect personal information from children, because the app does not collect personal information for the developer at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and the date at the top will change. This page is the notice. Dilo has no account and no mailing list, so there is no separate email about the change.
